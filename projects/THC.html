Project: Personal Logo

'You need a logo for people to remember you' one of my Lecturers once told a class I was sitting in. What a nightmare! But here we are, after countless tries, changes, and nothing that really convinced me. And if it doesn't really convince me, how should it convince anybody else?
But then finally it hit me like a cubic brick! And that's the whole story, really...



Logo Grid:

As the nature of my logo is quite static and geometrical, it made sense to build it on top of a custom grid. The grid inside the cube's faces is roughly spaced 1/2 x to give the Letters a balanced look and feel. The overall form is of course a hexagon shape which breaks down into 3 Symmetrical 'perspectively' distorted squares. A geometrically very interesting figure!



Spacing & Measurements:

The Logo spacing is rather simple, but why should it have to be complicated anyway? The line weight is defined as x and is used to define the safe space around the logo. The safe space is 16x16 X wide and forms a perfect square!

Logo Animation:

Since I do love animation and needed to show this logo in my showreels, I also decided to create some small intro and outro animations for my portfolio showreels :)

Workflow:
