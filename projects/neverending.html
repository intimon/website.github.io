Project: The Never Ending Story

Redesign of a Book Series by famous german author Michael Ende. While the fantasy-based stories are targeted at teens, his stories have become an absolute classic for everyone that grew up with it.

The Cover redesign and typography and typesetting is done in German!



Book Cover Design:

"The Never Ending Story" Book redesigned cover. The Book, that used to be targeted mainly for children, now targets the grown up fans of the classic. The design Concept can be further used for a whole collection series of books from Michael Ende - like Momo which is visible in the advertisment below!



Magazine Advertisment:

This Mock-Up shows a concept for a Magazine advert for the book series. Like the Book Cover, it keeps the visuals rather classic and ecstatic embracing the classic and collector form factor that is the selling point to the target group.
