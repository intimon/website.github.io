MoCap Issue Detection AI

This Machine Learning AI was made and trained on Motion Capture Data! The purpose of of this AI is to speed up the MoCap clean up process for the Artist and have the AI and mark issues in the Motion Caturing.

Datasets:

The data has been gathered from Motion Capture data that we recorded in Abertay Vipre Mocap Vacilities. This provided a solid base for our project but it would be benefitial to add more data from different sources!

Further work:

The training of the AI was successful and will be further improved in future. Next steps are to transform this AI into a tool accessible in different 3D Animation Software for Artist use!
